Derived from the 60 arc-second Ice Surface grid, EGM2008 elevations in meters.
Every sixth grid cell sampled; rounded to meters and gzip-packed. Ocean mask
derived by connectivity. No invented terrain. See terrain/etopo2022-6min.json
